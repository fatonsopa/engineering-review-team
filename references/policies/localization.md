# Localization review policy

Package default under the shared [Review Policy (POL)](../review_policy.md). Apply established project rules and valid customizations through the shared authority procedure.

## Purpose and scope

Require evidence-backed review of text integrity and applicable locale behavior across affected interfaces, data paths and communications. These obligations govern review practice; they do not establish new product markets or certify translation quality.

## Definitions

**Internationalization** enables software to accommodate languages and regional conventions. **Localization** adapts it for a particular audience. A **locale** supplies conventions; it does not alone determine residence, transaction currency or time zone. **Linguistic review** assesses meaning and language in context with stated competence and evidence. Shared definitions govern project rules, defaults, inferences, replacements and waivers.

## Principles

Preserve user meaning and value identity. Judge actual commitments and consequences, not a universal multilingual checklist. Keep technical evidence, rendered observations and linguistic judgments distinct. Respect legitimate alternatives and preserve uncertainty.

## Policy obligations

- **LOC-POL-01 — Establish effective requirements.** Use captured project criteria and selected package defaults through shared [Review scope and criteria](../intent_context.md#review-scope-and-criteria). Identify supported/planned locales, scripts, channels and relevant value contracts. Missing optional guidance is neither a defect nor authority to invent support.
- **LOC-POL-02 — Preserve text and value meaning.** Assess accepted characters, identifiers, amounts and temporal values across affected boundaries. An English-only interface does not justify corrupting accepted names or changing transaction currency.
- **LOC-POL-03 — Apply controls proportionately.** Select checks by the changed behavior, support commitments and user impact. Do not demand extraction of every literal, a fixed expansion percentage, every locale/category or an enterprise translation platform.
- **LOC-POL-04 — Preserve context and delivery.** Review relevant locale selection, message structure, resource lookup and recipient context through affected requests, jobs and release boundaries. Established alternatives remain valid; no universal preference precedence or fallback policy applies.
- **LOC-POL-05 — Preserve usable localized output.** Assess relevant scripts, expansion, direction and meaningful content in supported journeys. A different line break is not itself a defect; lost information or an inaccessible action needs a supported consequence.
- **LOC-POL-06 — Bound conclusions to evidence.** Separate source inspection, supplied/executed behavior, image viewing and linguistic assessment. Record actual locales and methods, uncertainty and coverage limits. A passed compiler, pseudolocale or plausible translation cannot establish full linguistic or market acceptance.

## Responsibilities and compliance

Localization owns its assigned investigation and returns supported findings and criterion assessments. The lead owns discovery, capture, routing, authority, acceptance and synthesis. Developers own corrections and separately authorized verification. Actual project owners decide product support and translation acceptance under their established authority; package role names do not appoint those owners.

Use the shared report and evidence contracts. Inspect only assigned sources and supplied observations through permitted read-only tools. Route required testing or language expertise through the lead. Preserve independently supported findings when another comparison is incomplete.

## Exceptions

Use the shared customization procedure for `localization`. Bind actual authority, scope, displaced controls or explicit removal, effective period/end conditions and consequences. A valid scoped replacement changes the mapped rule; unrelated text-integrity, evidence and report obligations remain. A waiver records the retained contradiction and residual consequence, not conformance.

Pending, expired, superseded or out-of-scope decisions supply no current exception. Preserve valid user authorization without inventing approval paperwork. Unresolved conflicts go to the lead. Host restrictions and evidence honesty cannot be replaced by a project translation preference.

## Ownership, review, and related standards

The package maintainer owns these defaults and their validation. Revisit affected guidance when supported runtimes, message formats, locale data, shared contracts or observed missed defects/false positives change; no invented calendar cadence applies.

Keep the [Localization Technical Standard (STD)](../standards/localization.md), [procedure](../domains/localization.md), shared [Review Technical Standard (STD)](../review_standard.md) and [project-intent guidance](../intent_context.md) consistent. Document validity does not prove reviewer behavior, native activation or product conformance.
