# Compliance review policy

Package default under the shared [Review Policy (POL)](../review_policy.md). Apply established project rules and valid customizations through the shared authority procedure.

## Purpose and scope

Require evidence-backed review of changed engineering behavior against applicable third-party rights, privacy, contracts and regulatory controls. These obligations govern review practice; they do not establish legal applicability or grant certification.

## Definitions

An **established obligation** has an identified source, actual scope and relevant authority. **Technical conformance** compares implementation with that obligation; it does not resolve disputed legal interpretation. **Provenance** identifies where material came from and the terms governing its use. **Personal data** follows the applicable definition; aliases, hashes and indirect identifiers are not automatically anonymous. Shared definitions govern defaults, project rules, replacements and waivers.

## Principles

Follow actual obligations and consequences. Keep technical evidence separate from interpretation, organizational approval and certification. Preserve legitimate alternatives and uncertainty. Small scope reduces irrelevant work, not obligations that still apply.

## Policy obligations

- **CMP-POL-01 — Establish scope and authority.** Use captured project criteria and compatible package defaults through shared [Review scope and criteria](../intent_context.md#review-scope-and-criteria). Identify applicable terms, uses, data flows, roles, contracts and effective decisions. Missing optional documentation does not itself establish a defect or an exemption.
- **CMP-POL-02 — Respect third-party rights.** Assess actual provenance, terms, notices and distribution or network-use conditions. Do not universally prohibit copyleft, treat a scanner label as permission, or assume a project license covers every included asset.
- **CMP-POL-03 — Preserve the agreed data lifecycle.** Assess collection, use, sharing, retention, rights and disposal against established purposes and requirements. Preserve legitimate holds and necessary retained records while preventing unrelated continued use. Consent is one possible basis or preference mechanism, not a universal remedy.
- **CMP-POL-04 — Make required controls observable.** Inspect applicable audit records and meaningful verification of the changed behavior. Preserve required attribution without collecting unnecessary sensitive payloads. A configured control is not evidence that every operational instance ran.
- **CMP-POL-05 — Bound conclusions and escalation.** Report supported technical contradictions, missing evidence and unresolved interpretation separately. Seek qualified interpretation through the lead when it changes the result. Do not invent a legal breach, framework exemption, assessor approval or organization-wide compliance claim.

## Responsibilities and compliance

Compliance owns its assigned investigation and evidence-backed return. The lead owns discovery, capture, routing, authority and synthesis. Developers own corrections and separately authorized verification. Actual legal, privacy, licensing or business owners retain their established authority; the package cannot appoint them or substitute a role label for approval.

Use the shared report and evidence contracts. Inspect assigned sources through permitted read-only tools. Request missing terms, decisions or verification through the lead and retain supported partial results.

## Exceptions

Use the shared customization procedure for `compliance`, binding actual authority, scope, displaced controls or explicit removal, effective period/end conditions and consequences. A valid replacement changes its mapped internal review rules. It cannot establish that an independent license, contract or statutory obligation ceased to apply. Record that unresolved comparison rather than silently accepting or overruling it.

A waiver preserves an unmet requirement and residual consequence; it is not conformance. Pending, expired, superseded or out-of-scope decisions supply no current exception. Preserve valid user authority without inventing approval paperwork. Host restrictions and evidence honesty remain binding.

## Ownership, review, and related standards

The package maintainer owns defaults and validation. Revisit affected controls when actual terms, applicable obligations, data flows, shared contracts or observed missed defects/false positives change. Do not impose an invented review cadence or require every review to research all laws.

Keep the [Compliance Technical Standard (STD)](../standards/compliance.md), [procedure](../domains/compliance.md), shared [Review Technical Standard (STD)](../review_standard.md) and [project-intent guidance](../intent_context.md) consistent. Document checks do not prove reviewer behavior, host activation or legal compliance.
